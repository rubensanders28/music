Timing calibration

Headphones (especially Bluetooth) and some speakers play sound a little late, which would make every tap look late. Calibrate once per set of headphones or speakers: you'll hear 4 count-in clicks, then tap along with the next 8.

How to count: say the beat numbers out loud, and "&" for the half-beats in between: "1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &". Sixteenths are counted "1 e & a". Tap on the notes, not the rests, but keep counting through the rests.
Keys: any key taps (Space is easiest). Enter starts a new try.
A take counts as played well when every note is in time (green) and there are no extra taps.
