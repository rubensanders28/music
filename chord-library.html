My chords

Every chord is built from its root by stacking notes at fixed distances. Those distances are the chord's formula, and they're the same whatever the root: learn the formula once and you can build the chord on any note. A half step (H) is one semitone: the very next key on a piano, or one fret on a guitar. A whole step (W) is two. The numbers (1, 3, 5…) say which note of the root's major scale each chord note is; a ♭ makes it a half step lower, a ♯ a half step higher.
