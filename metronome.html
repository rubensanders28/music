    Your tempo changes will show up here.

    How to use it: pick a chord switch (or "Just the metronome" for anything else, like a scale or a riff). Set the start tempo where you can play it perfectly, and the goal where you want to end up. Play it once through, then press Clean or Mistake. Be honest: practising mistakes at speed only makes them stick.
    Clean in a row: with 3×, you step up only after three clean takes in a row at a tempo; a mistake starts the count again. Your best clean tempo is the fastest one you've played cleanly that many times in a row, so it's a tempo you can really play.
    Each chord switch remembers its own start and goal, and keeps a history: one line per day, with the fastest tempo you played it cleanly.
    In automatic mode the tempo rises on its own every few bars; press Mistake whenever it gets messy (you then get the full number of bars again before it rises), and Slower when it's too fast.
    Keys: Space start/stop · ↑ clean · ↓ mistake · ← slower.
    Gap click: the click goes silent for a bar or two while you keep playing (and counting in your head). When it comes back, you should be exactly with it. If you've drifted ahead you're rushing; if it comes in before you, you're dragging. Start with short gaps at a slow tempo.
