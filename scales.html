Positions: guitarists learn a scale as a few shapes that each fit under your hand without moving it much. Seven-note scales use three notes on each string; pentatonic and blues scales use two (the famous "boxes"). Learn one position, then the next one up the neck: together they cover the whole fretboard.
Scale degrees number the notes from the root. A ♭ means one semitone lower than in the major scale, so ♭3 is what makes a scale sound minor.
