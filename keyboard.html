Keyboard

See any scale or chord on the keyboard, hear it, and play it yourself: on the screen, with your computer keys, or on a real keyboard connected with a USB cable.

Computer keys: A S D F G H J K are the white keys from C, W E T Y U the black ones; Z and X move an octave down or up. Hold several keys for a chord.
Degrees number the notes from the root, compared with the major scale: ♭3 is a semitone lower than the 3rd of the major scale, which is what makes a chord or scale minor.
Inversions: the same chord with a different note at the bottom. In first inversion the 3rd is the lowest note, in second inversion the 5th. Pianists use them to move between chords with small hand movements.
